How we can help

A few hours a week or daily support. Care is planned around the person, their routines and what matters to them.

Elderly and home care

Personal care, companionship, mobility support, medication reminders, respite for family carers, and support after a hospital stay.

Children with disabilities

Patient, attentive care and respite at home, so children are well supported and parents get time to rest.

Support for new mothers

Help at home after birth: rest, night support, feeding support and settling into newborn routines.

Clinical postnatal care is provided only by registered midwives.

Home nursing

Wound care, injections, vital-signs checks and care after surgery, at home.

Provided only by licensed nurses.

Family updates and coordination

Regular updates and photos, an agreed care plan, and help arranging appointments, so the whole family knows how things are going.

Caregiver training

Short courses in basic caregiving, dementia care, infection control, safe moving and handling, and first aid.

Living abroad? We'll look after your parents at home.

Whether you're in the UK, Canada, the US or elsewhere, we can be your hands at home in Nigeria, and keep you closely informed.

  • WhatsApp updates and photos after visits
  • A care plan you agree on and can review at any time
  • Help arranging appointments, errands and hospital visits
  • Health information shared only with written consent

The care we'd want for our own parents

Heedwell began with our CEO's work coordinating home care teams in Canada. She saw how much families valued reliable care at home, and clear updates from people they could trust, and wanted Nigerian families everywhere to have the same.

Today we bring that model home: trained caregivers, supervised by a care manager, with families kept informed. We think good care is in the small things: arriving on time, listening, noticing changes. That's what kindness in every detail means to us.

Fatima Zahrau Abdulrahman Co-Founder and CEO
